# Sales & Marketing Alignment

A handoff is not alignment.

If Marketing celebrates MQLs while Sales ignores them, the process is broken.

## Shared view

Marketing and Sales should agree on:

- ICP
- Priority segments
- Priority accounts
- Qualification
- Funnel stages
- Follow-up expectations
- Reasons for rejection
- Pipeline targets
- What feedback gets returned

## Operating questions

Every week:

- Which accounts are moving?
- Which new opportunities were created?
- Which campaigns influenced them?
- Which leads were rejected and why?
- Where are deals getting stuck?
- What is Sales hearing that Marketing needs to know?

## Handoff principle

The question should not be:

**“Did Marketing send the lead?”**

It should be:

**“What is the next best commercial action for this account?”**
