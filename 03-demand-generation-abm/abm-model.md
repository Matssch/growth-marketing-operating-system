# ABM Model

I would not start with 1:1 ABM everywhere.

Personalisation is expensive. It should follow commercial value.

## 1:Many

Used for a broad ICP or segment.

Examples:

- Segment-specific landing pages
- Paid campaigns
- SEO / use-case content
- Email nurture
- Customer proof by use case

## 1:Few

Used for clusters of high-value accounts with similar needs.

Examples:

- Industry-specific campaigns
- Named-account paid targeting
- Custom webinars or roundtables
- Tailored customer proof
- Coordinated SDR outreach

## 1:1

Used selectively for strategic accounts.

Examples:

- Account research
- Buying-group mapping
- Account-specific proposition
- Bespoke content or business case
- Executive outreach
- Coordinated Marketing + Sales sequence

## Principle

**The level of personalisation should increase with expected account value and buying probability.**

ABM is not personalisation for the sake of personalisation.

It is a resource-allocation decision.
