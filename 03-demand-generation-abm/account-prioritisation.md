# Account Prioritisation

Fit tells us **who we want**.

Intent and engagement tell us **who may be moving**.

I combine both.

## Account priority model

```text
Priority = ICP Fit + Intent + Engagement + Commercial Potential
```

Each input is scored from 1–5.

### Fit

How closely does the company match the ICP?

### Intent

Are there signs the account is actively researching the problem or category?

### Engagement

Is the account interacting with FlowOps across meaningful touchpoints?

### Commercial potential

What is the likely account value if won?

## What the score changes

The score should trigger a different action.

| Priority | Action |
|---|---|
| High | Sales + Marketing coordinated play |
| Medium | Segment nurture + targeted paid |
| Low | Scalable demand generation |
| Poor fit | Suppress or deprioritise |

Scoring only matters if it changes what the team does next.
