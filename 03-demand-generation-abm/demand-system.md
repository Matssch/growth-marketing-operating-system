# Demand System

## Demand creation

The buyer is relevant, but not actively looking.

Goal:

**Build memory, relevance and problem awareness.**

Possible plays:

- Category / problem content
- LinkedIn
- Customer proof
- Partnerships
- Events
- Thought leadership
- Target-account campaigns
- Sales-led outreach

## Demand capture

The buyer is already showing intent.

Goal:

**Make FlowOps easy to find, understand and choose.**

Possible plays:

- Paid search
- SEO
- Comparison pages
- Use-case pages
- Retargeting
- High-intent landing pages
- Demo / trial journeys

## Demand conversion

Interest exists, but the next step is weak.

Goal:

**Reduce friction and increase confidence.**

Possible plays:

- Message testing
- Social proof
- Landing-page CRO
- Better forms
- Clearer handoff
- Nurture
- Faster Sales follow-up

## Rule

I would not judge demand creation and demand capture using the same CPL target.

They have different jobs.

The shared question is whether they improve the probability of future commercial growth.
