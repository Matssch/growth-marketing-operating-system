# Performance Diagnostics

When performance moves, I diagnose before changing things.

## Example sequence

### CPA increased

Do not immediately cut spend.

Check:

1. CPC — did traffic get more expensive?
2. CTR — did message relevance change?
3. CVR — did landing-page performance drop?
4. Mix — did spend shift into a different audience?
5. Quality — are the new customers or opportunities actually better?
6. Tracking — did measurement change?

## Diagnostic tree

```text
CPA up
├── CPC up
│   ├── competition
│   ├── audience saturation
│   └── relevance
└── CVR down
    ├── landing page
    ├── audience quality
    ├── offer
    ├── device / UX
    └── tracking
```

The metric is the symptom.

The job is to find the cause.
