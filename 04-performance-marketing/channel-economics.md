# Channel Economics

Cheap traffic is not automatically efficient growth.

I evaluate channels at three levels.

## Media efficiency

- CPM
- CTR
- CPC

## Conversion efficiency

- Landing-page CVR
- CPL
- Cost per qualified lead

## Commercial efficiency

- Cost per opportunity
- CAC
- Pipeline / spend
- Revenue / spend
- Payback

## Example

| Channel | Spend | Leads | CPL | Opportunities | Cost / Opp | Pipeline |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Paid Search | €30K | 300 | €100 | 45 | €667 | €675K |
| LinkedIn | €30K | 150 | €200 | 40 | €750 | €800K |

LinkedIn looks worse on CPL.

It looks much closer when opportunity quality and pipeline are included.

That is why I would not reallocate budget based on CPL alone.
