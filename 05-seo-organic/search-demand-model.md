# Search Demand Model

I map search demand to the buying journey.

## Problem-aware

The buyer understands the problem but not the category.

Content role:

- Education
- Problem framing
- Category creation

## Solution-aware

The buyer is comparing ways to solve the problem.

Content role:

- Use cases
- Solution pages
- Product education

## Vendor-aware

The buyer is actively comparing providers.

Content role:

- Comparison pages
- Alternatives
- Customer proof
- Pricing / demo paths

## Rule

Traffic is useful.

**Qualified organic demand that moves toward a commercial action is better.**
