# Organic Conversion

SEO and CRO should not be managed as separate worlds.

For each important organic landing page I want to know:

- What was the search intent?
- Did the page answer it?
- What is the next logical action?
- Does the CTA match the visitor's level of intent?
- What happens after conversion?
- Does this traffic create qualified pipeline?

A high-ranking page with a weak journey leaves growth on the table.
