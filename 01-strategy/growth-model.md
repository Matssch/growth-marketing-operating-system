# Growth Model

## Start with the outcome

The annual growth marketing budget is **€300K**.

The primary target is **€3M in marketing-influenced pipeline**.

That gives the team a starting pipeline-to-spend ratio of:

**10x influenced pipeline / marketing spend**

That number is not automatically a success metric. Influenced pipeline can be overstated if attribution is weak.

The important part is that it gives us a commercial target to work backwards from.

## Work backwards

At an average contract value of €15K:

- €3M pipeline represents roughly 200 average-value opportunities in pipeline
- At a 25% opportunity-to-win rate, that would represent roughly 50 customers
- 50 customers × €15K = €750K in new ARR

These are planning assumptions, not promises.

As real conversion data becomes available, the model should be updated.

## Growth equation

I use a simple structure:

**Qualified Demand × Conversion × Customer Value = Growth**

That gives us three broad ways to improve performance:

### 1. Increase qualified demand

Reach more of the right companies.

Possible levers:

- Paid acquisition
- SEO
- Demand generation
- ABM
- Partnerships
- Content
- Events
- Referral

### 2. Improve conversion

Get more value from the demand already created.

Possible levers:

- Landing pages
- Message-market fit
- Forms
- Nurture
- Sales follow-up
- Onboarding
- CRO
- Customer proof

### 3. Improve customer value

Win better-fit customers and grow them over time.

Possible levers:

- Segment prioritisation
- Packaging
- Expansion
- Retention
- Cross-sell
- Account selection

## Funnel

```text
Target Market
    ↓
Engaged Account
    ↓
Lead / Buying Signal
    ↓
Qualified Opportunity
    ↓
Pipeline
    ↓
Customer
    ↓
Expansion
```

I would not force every buyer into the same funnel.

A high-intent inbound lead, an engaged target account and a self-serve signup can require completely different next actions.

The model should reflect buying behaviour, not make buying behaviour fit the dashboard.

## Growth levers

| Constraint | Evidence | Likely lever |
|---|---|---|
| Low awareness | Low direct/branded demand | Demand creation |
| Low high-intent traffic | Weak non-brand search / paid reach | SEO + paid |
| Weak landing-page conversion | Traffic strong, CVR weak | CRO |
| Poor lead quality | CPL strong, opportunity rate weak | ICP / targeting |
| Slow Sales follow-up | Good leads, low progression | Routing / SLA |
| Weak close rate | Pipeline exists, revenue weak | Proposition / Sales |
| Weak retention | Acquisition works, churn high | Lifecycle / product |

## My rule

**Find the constraint before adding more activity.**

More traffic is not a strategy when the page does not convert.

More leads are not a solution when Sales does not want them.

More campaigns are not growth when nobody can explain what they changed.
