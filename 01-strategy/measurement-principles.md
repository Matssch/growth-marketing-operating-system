# Measurement Principles

I do not want a reporting system that tells me everything.

I want one that tells me what needs attention.

## Four layers

### 1. Business outcomes

- Revenue
- ARR
- Pipeline
- Marketing-sourced pipeline
- Marketing-influenced pipeline
- CAC
- Payback

### 2. Funnel health

- Qualified opportunities
- Stage conversion
- Win rate
- Deal value
- Sales cycle
- Pipeline velocity

### 3. Channel performance

- Spend
- Traffic
- CPC
- Conversion rate
- CPL
- Cost per opportunity
- CAC
- Pipeline / spend

### 4. Diagnostic signals

- CTR
- Landing-page engagement
- Search visibility
- Account engagement
- Email response
- Returning visitors
- Branded demand

Diagnostic metrics explain performance.

They should not replace commercial outcomes.

## Reporting format

For every important movement, I want four things:

**What changed?**

**Why do we think it changed?**

**What does it mean commercially?**

**What are we doing next?**

That turns reporting into management.

## Attribution

I would not pretend one attribution model can explain the entire buying journey.

I would use multiple views:

- First touch → where demand entered
- Lead creation → what created the conversion
- Opportunity creation → what happened around commercial intent
- Multi-touch / influence → which activity supported progression
- Account view → how the full buying group engaged

The goal is not attribution perfection.

The goal is to make better investment decisions with enough evidence.
