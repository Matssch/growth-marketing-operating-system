# 01 | Growth Strategy

Strategy is where I define the commercial problem before choosing channels.

The output of this section should make three things clear:

1. What the business is trying to achieve.
2. What needs to change to get there.
3. How Marketing will know whether it is working.

## Files

- `business-context.md` | starting point and commercial challenge
- `growth-model.md` | target, funnel logic and growth levers
- `measurement-principles.md` | how I keep reporting connected to decisions
