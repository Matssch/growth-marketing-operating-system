# Synthetic Data

The datasets in this folder are fictional.

They exist to make the frameworks testable and to support future SQL, dashboard and performance-analysis examples.

No confidential customer or employer data is included.

## Files

- `accounts.csv` | account fit, intent, engagement and tiering
- `campaigns.csv` | simplified channel performance
- `leads.csv` | lead source and opportunity progression
- `opportunities.csv` | pipeline and opportunity stage
