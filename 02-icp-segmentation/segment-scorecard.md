# Segment Scorecard

| Segment | Market Opportunity | Product Fit | Commercial Value | Ability to Win | Priority |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Growth-stage SMB | 4 | 4 | 3 | 5 | High |
| Mid-market | 5 | 5 | 5 | 4 | Highest |
| Enterprise | 3 | 5 | 5 | 2 | Selective |

## Decision

**Mid-market becomes the primary growth segment.**

It combines strong product fit, meaningful deal value and a realistic buying motion.

**SMB remains a scalable demand-capture segment.**

The economics can work, but the Sales model should stay lighter.

**Enterprise becomes selective rather than broad.**

The potential value is high, but long cycles and higher acquisition effort make broad enterprise targeting inefficient at this stage.

This is where ABM starts to become useful.
