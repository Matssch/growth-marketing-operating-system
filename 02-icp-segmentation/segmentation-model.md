# Segmentation Model

I use segmentation to connect broad market strategy with specific execution.

The goal is not to create ten personas with different stock photos.

The goal is to group companies that have similar economics, problems and buying behaviour.

## Example segments

### Segment A | Growth-stage SMB

- 50–249 employees
- Faster buying cycle
- Lower ACV
- More digital / self-directed research
- Strong fit for scalable demand capture and lifecycle

### Segment B | Mid-market

- 250–999 employees
- Multiple stakeholders
- Higher operational complexity
- Larger commercial value
- Hybrid digital + Sales motion

### Segment C | Enterprise

- 1,000+ employees
- Longer buying cycle
- Larger buying group
- High potential value
- Stronger need for ABM and coordinated Sales activity

## Segment score

I would prioritise segments using:

**Market Opportunity × Product Fit × Commercial Value × Ability to Win**

Then validate the score with real performance.

The strategy should change when the data proves the assumptions wrong.

That is the point of having a model in the first place.
