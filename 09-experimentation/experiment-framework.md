# Experiment Framework

## Observation

What is happening?

Use evidence.

## Hypothesis

Why do we think it is happening?

Format:

**If we [change], then [metric] will improve because [reason].**

## Test

What is the smallest credible way to test the hypothesis?

## Primary metric

Which metric determines success?

## Guardrail

Which metric should not become materially worse?

## Decision

One of three outcomes:

**Scale** — evidence is strong enough to expand.

**Improve** — direction is promising, but the test needs another iteration.

**Kill** — evidence does not justify more time or budget.

Killing weak ideas early is part of growth, not a failed process.
