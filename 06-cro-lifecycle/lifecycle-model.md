# Lifecycle Model

Growth does not stop when a lead converts.

## Journey

```text
Lead
 ↓
Qualified
 ↓
Opportunity
 ↓
Customer
 ↓
Activated
 ↓
Retained
 ↓
Expanded
 ↓
Advocate
```

## Lifecycle plays

### Lead → Opportunity

- Behaviour-based nurture
- Stronger proof
- Use-case content
- Sales alerts
- Retargeting

### Customer → Activated

- Onboarding
- Product education
- Success milestones
- Usage triggers

### Activated → Retained

- Adoption campaigns
- Value reminders
- Customer education
- Risk signals

### Retained → Expanded

- Cross-sell
- Upsell
- New use cases
- Account expansion

### Customer → Advocate

- Reviews
- Referral
- Customer stories
- Events
- Peer proof

Acquisition without retention creates a very expensive treadmill.
